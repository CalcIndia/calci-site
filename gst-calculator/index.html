How this calculator works

GST works the same way for any price.

1
Add GST

Multiply the price by the GST rate and add it on. ₹1,000 at 18% becomes ₹1,180.

2
Remove GST

Divide the full price by 1 plus the rate. ₹1,180 ÷ 1.18 = ₹1,000 before GST.

3
Split it

Within a state, half is CGST and half is SGST. Between states, it is all IGST.

GST slabs from 22 September 2025

GST 2.0 cut the main slabs to 5% and 18%, with a special 40% rate for luxury and sin goods. The old 12% and 28% slabs were removed. Gold stays at 3%, and many essentials are nil-rated.

Common questions

Quick answers about GST rates and how to work them out.

?How do I calculate GST on a price?

Multiply the price by the GST rate. For ₹5,000 at 18%, GST is ₹900 and the total is ₹5,900.

?How do I remove GST from a total?

Divide the total by 1 plus the rate. For ₹5,900 at 18%: ₹5,900 ÷ 1.18 = ₹5,000 before GST, so GST is ₹900.

?What are the GST rates in 2026?

The main rates are 5% and 18%, with a special 40% rate for luxury and sin goods. Some essentials are 0%, and gold and jewellery are taxed at 3%.

?What are CGST, SGST and IGST?

For a sale within a state, GST is split equally into CGST (central) and SGST (state). For a sale to another state, the whole GST is IGST.

?Were the 12% and 28% slabs removed?

Yes. From 22 September 2025, most items moved from 12% to 5% and from 28% to 18%. A few luxury and sin goods moved to 40%.

?How do I find the GST rate for my item?

Check the GST rate for its HSN code (goods) or SAC code (services) on the GST portal or in the CBIC rate list, or ask your accountant.
