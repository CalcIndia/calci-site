Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what information CalC (calci.co.in) collects, how it is used and the choices you have. By using this website you agree to this policy.

The numbers you enter

All calculations happen inside your own browser. The amounts, rates and other values you type into a calculator are not sent to us, and we do not store them on any server.

Information stored on your device

We save your light or dark mode choice in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it on your next visit. This is not used to identify you, and you can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

Visitor statistics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics, such as Cloudflare Web Analytics, to count page visits and see which calculators are popular. These tools show us totals, such as the number of visits, and do not identify individual visitors.

Advertising

We may show advertisements from Google AdSense to keep CalC free.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.

To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Hosting and security logs

Like all websites, our hosting provider may record basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, to keep the site secure and working. We do not use this to identify you.

Emails you send us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We do not sell or share them.

Children

CalC is meant for adults planning their finances. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Links to other websites

Our pages may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Changes to this policy
