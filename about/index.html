About CalC

CalC is a free collection of money calculators for people in India.

Why CalC exists

Most financial calculators online are either hard to read or built to sell you a product. CalC is different: each calculator does one job well, explains the result in plain English and never asks for your details.

How the calculators work

Each calculator uses standard financial formulas and works through your plan month by month. Everything runs inside your own browser, so the numbers you type are not sent anywhere.

The results are estimates. They assume a steady return and do not include taxes or fund charges, so real results will differ. Please read the disclaimer before acting on any result.

What is coming next

We are adding new calculators and updating rates and tax rules regularly. If there is a calculator you would like to see, tell us on the contact page.

Found a mistake?

We check every calculator carefully, but if something looks wrong, please let us know and we will fix it quickly.
